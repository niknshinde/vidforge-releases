Everything on one timeline.

Edit by hand, by prompt, or both at once. The AI works on the same project you see on screen.

01

Search your footage

Find the shot by describing it. Media is analysed on your machine so clips are searchable by what is in them.

02

Drive it from your AI

A built-in MCP server lets Claude Code, Codex and other clients cut, arrange, caption and render for you.

03

Captions

Generate captions from the audio and place them on their own track, ready to restyle.

04

Voiceover

Write a line, get a voice. Use a stock voice or clone your own through a cloud provider.

05

Motion graphics

Titles, lower thirds and animated layers with transitions between clips.

06

Updates itself

New versions arrive in the app. No hunting for installers after the first download.

From idea to export.

Three steps, and the first one is a download.

Install

Run the installer. It sets up for your user account and needs no admin rights.

Connect

Add your media, then connect your AI client from the settings screen.

Direct

Describe the cut. Watch the timeline change. Fix what you like by hand, then render.

Get VidForge.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Needs about 2 GB of disk space once installed.
